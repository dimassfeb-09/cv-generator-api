Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 3, 2026

Introduction

At CVGen, we believe that your professional data belongs to you. This policy outlines how we handle your information with the highest standards of transparency and security during your resume-building journey.

Data We Collect

To generate your professional PDF documents, we process information you explicitly provide:

  • Personal Details: Name, email, phone number, and location.
  • Professional History: Work experience, education, and technical skills.
  • Social Profiles: Links to LinkedIn, GitHub, or your personal portfolio.

Encryption & Storage

Your data is treated with enterprise-grade protection:

  • End-to-End Security: All data transmissions are encrypted via SSL/TLS protocols.
  • Encrypted Database: Text information is stored in encrypted PostgreSQL databases hosted by Supabase.
  • Private Storage: Your generated PDF files are stored in private cloud buckets with time-limited access tokens.

Your Control

We provide you with absolute control over your data. You have the right to:

  • Request a Complete Deletion of your account and all associated CV data.
  • Access and download your data snapshots at any time.
  • Request a verification code via email for any sensitive data removal.

Third-Party Services

We only use trusted infrastructure providers to power our API:

  • Supabase for secure database and file storage.
  • Vercel for high-performance serverless computing.
  • Gmail API for secure verification and notifications.

Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding your privacy or data security, please reach out to our team at buildwithdimas@gmail.com.
